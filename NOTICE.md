# Sources and attribution

The four document-type skills (`write-tutorial`, `write-how-to`, `write-reference`, and `write-explanation`) adapt the documentation principles in
[Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) by **Daniele Procida**, licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: condensed the framework into four independent workflows; added practical
checks, source-handling rules, community-derived observations, invented examples,
and Simplified Chinese adaptation guidance. This collection is not an official
Diátaxis release and does not imply endorsement.

The source website's original text is English; Polish is a separate translation.
The research notes came from the local Diátaxis translation project. We retain
citations instead of bundling a scraped website or copying whole discussions.

Skill-level attribution remains inside every writing skill when installed alone.
Community experiences are attributed opinions, not verified causal claims.

`write-clearly` adapts material from [Google Technical Writing courses](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing/overview), created and shared by Google under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It condenses the guidance and adds Chinese adaptation and safeguards for uncertain technical meaning. Source and license checked 2026-11-03.

`write-clearly`, `write-error-messages`, and `write-accessible-content` adapt ideas from Google's Technical Writing, Writing Helpful Error Messages, and Technical Writing for Accessibility courses. Google shares course content under CC BY 4.0; code samples may be separately licensed under Apache 2.0 and media may have separate terms. These skills summarize guidance without reproducing course examples or media. Source and license checked 2026-11-03.
